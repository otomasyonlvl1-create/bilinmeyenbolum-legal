Bilinmeyen Bölüm Automation is a tool for managing and publishing interactive story videos on authorized social media accounts.

Depending on platform permissions, the application may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, video metadata, publication status, engagement metrics, and public comments needed to evaluate audience votes.

Information is used to authenticate accounts, schedule and publish videos, evaluate A/B audience choices, prevent duplicate posts, and generate reports.

4. Storage and Security

Application data is intended to be stored locally on an authorized computer. Access tokens and other credentials must be protected against unauthorized access.

5. Third-Party Platforms

The application interacts with TikTok and may integrate with Instagram and YouTube. Data access is subject to each platform's permissions and policies.
