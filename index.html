Bilinmeyen Bölüm Automation

Interactive Story Publishing & Management

About the Application

Bilinmeyen Bölüm Automation is a Windows-based content management application being developed to schedule and publish original interactive story videos on authorized social media accounts.

How It Works

The application is designed to manage existing video libraries, schedule publications, and evaluate audience A/B choices to determine the next episode of each interactive story.

TikTok integration is planned through official APIs and account authorization.
